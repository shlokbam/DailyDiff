**What Happened:** **You can protect your deployment pipelines from supply chain attacks by pinning your GitHub Actions to immutable commit SHAs instead of mutable version tags.** When you use a tag like `@v4`, an attacker who compromises the repository could silently update that tag to point to malicious code.
