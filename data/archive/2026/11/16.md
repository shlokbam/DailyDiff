### [Cool Tool] Swap `cat` for `less` to Browse Large Files Without Freezing
**Use `less` instead of `cat` when opening big files to avoid terminal freezes and gain instant search.** `cat` streams the whole file at once and offers no search capability, which can hang the screen on large logs. `less` lets you scroll (`SPACE`, `b`, `g`, `G`), search (`/keyword`), and quit (`q`), making log inspection far smoother.

**Why it matters:** Recommends using `less` over `cat` for viewing large files to prevent terminal freezing and enable text searching, a simple but effective productivity boost for daily Linux operations.

**Who cares:** Junior developers and DevOps engineers working extensively in Linux terminal environments.
