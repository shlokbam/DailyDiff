### [Watchlist] Schema Drift vs. Semantic Drift in Data Pipelines
**Schema drift is easy to spot, but semantic drift silently corrupts data pipelines.** The article explains that while missing columns or type changes cause immediate failures, a field that keeps its name and type but changes meaning (semantic drift) can pass unnoticed. The author built a lab introducing nine failure types and tested four detectors, showing many pipelines miss semantic issues, leading to hidden data quality problems.

**Why it matters:** Explains the difference between schema drift and semantic drift, exposing a blind spot where pipelines silently accept wrong business meaning and corrupt data.

**Who cares:** Data engineers and ML pipeline owners who need robust data‑quality safeguards.
