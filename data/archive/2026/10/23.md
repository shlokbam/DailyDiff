### [Cool Tool] TwinDesk
**TwinDesk lets you share screens, apps, clipboard, and input devices across Mac, Windows, Linux, and Android without any cloud or account.** It’s an open‑source solution that synchronises displays, files, calls, texts and notifications, turning a heterogeneous hardware setup into a single seamless desk.

**Why it matters:** Enables seamless cross-device workflow integration without cloud dependencies, improving productivity for developers juggling multiple OS environments.

**Who cares:** Full-stack developers, DevOps engineers, and power users managing heterogeneous hardware stacks.

**Verdict:** `INTEGRATE` | [Source](https://github.com/InukaAsith/TwinDesk)

**Why it matters:** Provides a self‑hosted, local‑first alternative to commercial SaaS task managers, ensuring data sovereignty and eliminating subscription lock‑in for teams.

**Who cares:** DevOps engineers, privacy‑conscious teams, and organizations seeking self‑hosted productivity infrastructure.
